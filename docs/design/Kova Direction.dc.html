Kova · Product direction

Home Assistant shows you your devices. Kova shows you your home’s day.

HA is built around entities: every device becomes a card, and every behaviour becomes a separate trigger-condition-action rule. v2 of our prototype copied that shape. People don’t think in entities, though. They think in time (“after dark”, “at bedtime”, “after Fajr”) and in states of the house (“everyone’s asleep”, “guests are over”). Kova should be built on those two things.

01

The Home Timeline

One horizontal line is the home screen. The past shows what happened and why. “Now” is the present state. The future shows what the home will do next, scheduled and predicted. Scrub back to see last night, or drag a future event to reschedule it. In HA this is spread across four places (Overview, Logbook, History, the automation list); in Kova it’s one view.

02

Modes: the home has a state

Right now your home runs on 13 automations and 4 scripts, all of which set devices at a time or on an event. Almost all of them are really describing how the house should feel in a part of the day. Kova makes that the core idea. The home is always in one mode, each mode says how things should be, and moving between modes does the work. Scenes become temporary modes layered on top, and Away and Guests are modes too.
